The Reality in Kuwait

Why social media alone isn't enough

Most businesses in Kuwait rely purely on Instagram. Here is why that is costing you loyal customers every single day.

Customers search Google before they visit.

When residents search Google for "dentist in Salmiya" or "best salon near me", Instagram profiles rarely show up at the top. If you don't have a fast website, Google recommends your competitors instead.

An Instagram page alone looks unofficial.

Social media is cluttered. Pricing gets buried under old posts, highlights are difficult to navigate, and serious customers view a business without an official domain as temporary or unverified.

Without a site you lose bookings to competitors.

When customers can't find clear prices, working hours, or a direct 1-tap WhatsApp booking button within 5 seconds, they leave your page and book with another business down the street.
